# BART VDB

This is the website for my own business, BART VDB.

I originally built the site mainly around web development, but the idea has grown into something broader. I want to offer practical tech services for private customers and small businesses in Bergen, ranging from websites and custom web applications to PC help, IT support and basic security.

The main goal with the website is to keep everything simple and clear. No unnecessary technical language, no overcomplicated design and no pretending to be a massive IT company.

## What I offer

The business is built around a few main areas:

- Web development
- Business websites
- Custom web applications and internal systems
- PC and general IT help
- IT support for small businesses
- Practical IT security

I am especially interested in helping smaller companies that do not need, or cannot justify, having their own internal IT department.

## The idea behind the design

I wanted the site to feel personal, practical and slightly industrial.

The visual style uses:
- Dark navy
- Light blue
- Strong typography
- Simple layouts
- Minimal animation
- A bit of constructivist / Soviet-inspired graphic design

I deliberately avoided the typical modern tech-company look with glowing gradients, glass cards and generic cybersecurity graphics.

The idea is that the website should feel like there is an actual person behind the company.

## Projects

The site also contains some of the projects I have worked on.

### TotalTiming

TotalTiming is a work management system built for the construction industry.

It brings things like time registration, projects, absence and administration together in one system and is designed to work on both mobile and desktop.

This is one of the projects that pushed me further from only building websites into building complete systems.

### Totalentreprenør AS

A business website built for a Norwegian construction company, with focus on making the services easy to understand and making it simple for potential customers to get in contact.

## Why I built this

I am educated in front-end development, but I have always been interested in more than just the frontend.

I enjoy building things, figuring out why something does not work and finding a practical solution to it.

That can be a website, an internal system, a computer problem or something completely different within IT.

This website is basically the place where I bring all of that together.
